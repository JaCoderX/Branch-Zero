Rules kept (WORLD-3D §5): glTF 2.0 `.glb`, +Y up, 1 unit = 1 m, textures ≤ 1024² (the one texture is the 1024×512
character atlas, imported lossless with mipmaps — Basis Universal would blur the block edges and cost more bytes than the
79 KB PNG), no Mixamo. Retired 2026-09-07 (Stage 3): Kenney Mini Characters 1.0 (CC0, chibi) — see git history before
this note if they are ever wanted back. Not used: **Quaternius** — the pack pages still carry a CC0 badge, but the site's
licence page is the *Quaternius Asset License v1.0* (dated 2026-08-28), which forbids redistributing the assets "in
original or modified form" outside a completed product; committing raw `.glb` files to this public repo would sit on the
wrong side of that line, and the downloads are Google Drive folders, not direct files. Freesound (Kenney's CC0 packs
sufficed).
