The mesh, rig and the idle / run clips come from Kenney's "Animated Characters" packs; the skins in
Textures/staff_atlas.png are repainted from the Protagonists and Survivors skins. Both packs are CC0 1.0.
The walk / work / refuse clips and the bank wardrobe are D9 Studio work on top of that CC0 base
(apps/game/tools/bank_staff_rig.py, apps/game/tools/bank_staff_atlas.py) and are MIT with this repo.
