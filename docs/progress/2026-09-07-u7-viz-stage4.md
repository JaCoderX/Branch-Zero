## Approach — procedural modules, not a wall kit

The kickoff allowed wall modules under `assets/models/` and one wall atlas. Neither was needed. Every module is a
`BoxMesh` in a material the palette already had (`Marble`, `MarbleDark`, `Brass`, `Paper`), built by two new
functions in `scripts/bank_interior.gd`:

- **`shell(name, pos, size, face, spec)`** dresses one face of an existing wall box: marble **pilasters** (green base,
  brass fillet, brass capital) at given along-wall coordinates; a deep-green **frieze** with brass fillets at
  y 3.12–3.34, under the clerestory sills; raised **paper panels in brass frames** between the pilasters, or a frame
  alone around a board or plaque that is already there (payee list, service menu, limits poster, FX DESK); brass
  **stiles** splitting the wainscot into panels; and, for boxes `wall()` never built (the elevator shaft), a green
  **dado + brass cap** and a brass **crown**. `gaps` cut the frieze where a housing rises through it (ledger board,
  names board, vault frame, ACCOUNT OPENING plaque). Every hero prop, board and plaque on a face was measured first
  (`PropKit.aabb`) so nothing lands on the vault frame (x 5.5–10.5), the names-board housing (z −5.3 … −2.3), the
  windows (y ≥ 3.45), the call panel or the hall lantern.
- **`_coffers()`** hangs marble **beams with a brass fillet** 0.28 m under the ceiling slabs on lines chosen to miss the
  eight pendant canopies, the skylight well, the vault frame (top 4.45) and the ledger housing (top 4.44), and lines the
  skylight well with a green band under the brass curb. The skylight glass keeps `no_batch` and casts no shadow.

Faces dressed: north partition (both faces, both halves — the pilasters at x 5.75 / 10.25 turn the vault opening and
its lintel into a portal), north wall south face (manager's office and vault antechamber, flanking the frame),
west wall east face (counter backs, Name Desk), east wall west face, south wall north face in its two halves either
side of the entrance gap, and the elevator shaft's three lobby faces. **415 modules**, mesh only — no collider was
added or moved, so navigation, zones, door gaps, escort waypoints, NPC homes and teleports are byte-for-byte U3.

**MultiMesh — evaluated, not used.** `bake_static` already merges the benches (8 modules), lobby plants (3 + 4 small),
rope posts (3) and now the 415 shell pieces into one surface per material, i.e. **zero** draw calls beyond the 29
batch surfaces. A `MultiMeshInstance3D` per repeated prop cannot join that batch, so it would *add* `surfaces × 3
passes` draws (6–9 per prop type) to save ≈ 2k triangles of a 47.7k building. At these instance counts the bake beats
MultiMesh on the only gate that is tight (draw calls); no helper was added to `props.gd` so nothing unused ships.
