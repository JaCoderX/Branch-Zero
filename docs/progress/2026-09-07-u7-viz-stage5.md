## (1) HUD — a bottom band

`scripts/hud.gd`. Everything persistent now lives in the bottom quarter of the frame, where the camera sees floor:
passbook card bottom-left (zero-height anchor at the help line, grows upward with its lines, brass left edge, no wrap),
key legend along the very bottom (fades after 25 s; **F1** pins it back), `[E] Talk to …` prompt bottom-centre, zone chip
bottom-right. The passbook and chip step aside while a dialogue or form is open, because the dialogue box lands on the
same band. Stage lines and error toasts are six-second notices in the top-right corner, sized to their text, so they
never sit on the dialogue box. From the lobby camera spot the ledger board, the frieze, the VAULT plaque and the door
repeater are all clear ([lobby](./captures/2026-09-07-u7-viz5-lobby.jpg) vs Stage 4's). No `strings.json` change — the
help text is the same string, laid out in one line.

## (2) Camera — OTS arm + talk two-shot

`scripts/player.gd`, camera arm only. The spring arm sits **0.6 m off the right shoulder** (`CAM_SHOULDER`) at **−17°**
(was −22°), still 5 m, margin 0.3, colliding on layer 1 with the player excluded. Geometry note for the next agent: a
lateral offset alone cannot clear an NPC standing straight ahead at 2 m — the ray from the camera passes 0.16 m from the
player's axis, inside the 0.35 m capsule — so the fix the Stage 3 lesson asked for is a **two-shot**: while a dialogue is
open the arm eases (6 /s) to 3.8 m and the pivot swings **38°** off the player→NPC line (`TALK_SWING`), then eases back to
the player's own yaw on close. `main.gd` already calls `look_at_point(npc)` right before an NPC opens its dialogue;
the player stores that point and the `Dialogue.opened` / `closed` signals toggle the framing — no main.gd change. Mo, Dev
and Ruth all stand beside the player with the body visible in the new `viz_shots` views; the Stage 3 `*_close` views
still hide the body for silhouette checks. The shallower pitch shows the coffers in the wide lobby shots and the two
look-up presets (pitch −2° / −6°) show the skylight well and beams. F6 → E → Esc → F4 verified on the web build below.
Teleports, WASD, orbit and the escort are untouched; `set_view` snaps the eased yaw so a teleport never swings.

## (3) Particles — two CPU systems

`scripts/feel.gd`, a `Feel` node in `main.tscn` beside `Lighting` (outside `BankInterior`, so `bake_static` never sees it).
Both are `CPUParticles3D` (no GPU transform feedback, so identical on WebGL 2), cast no shadow, and draw one unshaded
billboard `QuadMesh` in a procedural radial `GradientTexture2D` — no texture file, no licence:

- **SkylightDust** — 96 slow motes in the sun shaft under the lobby skylight (x −6…6, z −4…5), 11 s life, 20 Hz sim,
  additive, alpha 0.24. Always on; the one system on the default lobby path.
- **StampInk** — 26-particle one-shot puff off the teller's stamp on the desk's `signing` stage (the same event
  `audio.gd` plays the stamp thud on); fires at Counter 1's stamp, or the manager's when the player is in his office.

Cost: +1 draw call each while visible, +2 materials (34 → **36** ≤ 40). Counted and asserted in `run_viz_budget`.

## (4) Fonts — Inter + Cinzel (OFL)

`assets/fonts/Inter.ttf` (variable `opsz`/`wght`, 877 KB) and `assets/fonts/Cinzel.ttf` (variable `wght`, 125 KB), both
fetched from google/fonts `main` with their `OFL.txt` beside them (`LICENSE-*-OFL.txt`), sha256 in
[`CREDITS.md`](../../CREDITS.md). `scripts/fonts.gd` (`BankFonts`) installs Inter as the **default theme font** — the one
place every Control (HUD, dialogue box, slips, the SubViewport boards, which a window Theme would not reach) and every
`Label3D` without its own font (nameplates, vault clock) falls back to — and puts Cinzel 700 on the **title plaques**
(`Label3D` with the `template` meta whose first word is all caps: VAULT, COUNTER 1, NAME DESK, ACCOUNT OPENING,
MANAGER'S OFFICE, ELEVATOR…, 13 of them). Body signs (limits poster, payee list, service menu, "Why do banks wait?") keep
Inter for legibility. One UI face + one plaque face, as the kickoff asked; the boards were not given a monospace.
Web check: prompt, passbook, dialogue choices, nameplates and the door clock all read on `:5174` at 1280×720.
